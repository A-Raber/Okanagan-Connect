Why register?

Attendees

  • Bookmark events and sync RSVPs to your calendar.
  • Get notified when hosts publish schedule changes.
  • Rate sessions to help curate future lineups.

Hosts

  • Publish events, manage RSVPs, and track vendor tasks.
  • Collaborate with campus teams using shared dashboards.
  • Export attendee lists into your planning tools.

Vendors

  • Respond to invites faster with pre-approved profiles.
  • Share setup requirements and documents in one place.
  • Access post-event feedback to improve future activations.
